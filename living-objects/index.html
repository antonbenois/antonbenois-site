Living Objects, 2021

Solo exhibition, BABEL Visningsrom for Kunst, Trondheim
Film, installation, objects

Living Objects developed from research in museums where the past is reconstructed through costumed employees, rebuilt environments and historical objects. Interviews and museum archives became the basis for a film and a group of works in which bodies, images and artefacts move between documentation and performance. The exhibition considers what happens when a person is asked to function as a historical object.
