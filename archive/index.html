Archive, 2026

Aluminium, cement, 27 photo-transfer print T-shirts
Fotobokfestival Oslo, Oslo Kunstforening
23–27 September 2026

Archive mutates Lost in Space into 27 double-sided T-shirts retaining the publication’s recto/verso structure. Each shirt carries a Soviet space-dog photograph on the front and the archival reverse of the same image on the back. The book moves into an in-between state: archive, body, clothing, evidence, merchandise and exhibition object at once.
